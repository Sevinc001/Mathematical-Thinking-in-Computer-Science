# Study Notes & Assignment Trackers (Modules 1 & 2)

## 1. Magic Squares (Sehrli Kvadratlar)
* **$3 \times 3$ Magic Square:** Arranging numbers from $1$ to $9$ such that rows, columns, and diagonals sum to $15$.
* **Narrowing the Search:** Logical proof showing why the center cell must always be $5$.
* **Multiplicative Magic Squares:** Grids where the product of elements in rows, columns, and diagonals are equal.

## 2. Integer Linear Combinations & Coin Puzzles
* **Different People Have Different Coins:** Solving Diophantine equations and GCD-based payment limits (e.g., coin values like 7 and 13).
* **Free Accommodation:** Mathematical modeling of room combinations and reachability.
