# Module 1 & 2: Proofs, Puzzles, Optimality, Recursion & Logic

This folder contains all theoretical notes, problem breakdowns, puzzle analyses, and Python implementations for the first two combined modules of the course.

---

## 📚 Detailed Table of Contents

### Part 1: Proofs & Existence (Module 1)
* **Proof Strategies:** 
  * Proof by example and constructing valid instances.
  * Impossibility proofs and reasoning with structural invariants.
* **Chessboard Tiling Puzzle:** Proving mathematically why tiling a mutilated chessboard (with missing corners of the same color) using dominoes is impossible.
* **Existence & Construction Puzzles:** 
  * Splitting shapes into congruent parts (e.g., octagon splitting).
  * Constructive proofs showing that a required configuration exists.

### Part 2: Magic Squares, Optimality & Computer Search (Module 1/2)
* **Magic Squares:** Multiplicative magic squares and integer linear combinations.
* **Optimality on Chessboards:** Finding the maximum number of rooks, knights, and bishops under specific board constraints.
* **Computer Search & Backtracking (Python Implementations):**
  * **N-Queens Puzzle:** Permutations, brute-force search, and optimized backtracking algorithms.
  * **16 Diagonals Puzzle:** Grid-based recursive search for non-overlapping diagonal placements.

### Part 3: Recursion & Induction (Module 2)
* **Recursion:** 
  * Solving coin exchange problems and understanding recursive structures.
  * Classic puzzles: Hanoi Towers, number guessing, and finding local maximums.
* **Mathematical Induction:** 
  * Arithmetic series, plane coloring, and compound interest.
  * Inequalities (Bernoulli's inequality, AM-GM) and strengthening inductive hypotheses.

### Part 4: Logic, Counterexamples & Pigeonhole Principle (Module 2)
* **Logic & Counterexamples:** Analyzing logical statements, evaluating conditional expressions, and "Always Prime" puzzles.
* **Reductio ad Absurdum & Pigeonhole Principle:** 
  * Proof by contradiction.
  * Distributing balls and numbers into boxes, antimagic squares, and handshakes principles.
